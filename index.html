Our Heritage

The Tummi Story

At Tummi, every single empanada is a tribute to authentic Peruvian culinary roots. Handcrafted with devotion, our creations combine time-honored recipes with a fresh, elevated perspective to bring you a truly extraordinary artisanal experience.

From our premium ingredients and warm spices to our signature windowless kraft packaging accented with terracotta details, every element is designed to honor our culture and give you the very best.
